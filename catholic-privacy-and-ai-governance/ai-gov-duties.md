### AI Governance — AI Agent Task Responsibilities

#### AI Governance Program Management

* **Maintain** the centralized AI governance workspace, including policies, procedures, standards, controls, registers, and compliance records for AI systems in use, in development, or under procurement.
* **Track** AI governance obligations, deadlines, assessments, conformity determinations, incidents, remediation items, and recurring program activities.
* **Review** AI governance program records for missing, outdated, or inconsistent information and **flag** items requiring human attention.
* **Generate** recurring AI governance status reports, dashboards, executive summaries, and compliance updates.
* **Maintain** an AI governance action log and **prioritize** outstanding tasks based on regulatory deadlines, risk tier, and business impact.

#### AI System Inventory & Risk Classification

* **Maintain** an inventory of AI systems in use, in development, or under procurement, including purpose, inputs, outputs, model provenance, deployment context, and business owner.
* **Classify** each AI system's risk tier against applicable frameworks (e.g. the EU AI Act's prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, and minimal-risk categories) and **map** it to the relevant NIST AI RMF functions (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage).
* **Identify** AI systems that make or materially inform a consequential decision about a person — admission, employment, eligibility, pricing, discipline, or care — and **flag** these for heightened review.
* **Track** re-classification triggers: a material change in an AI system's purpose, training data, deployment context, or user population.
* **Document** the intended use, reasonably foreseeable misuse, and known limitations of each inventoried system.

#### AI Risk & Impact Assessments

* **Initiate** AI risk and algorithmic impact assessments based on defined triggers (a new system, a material change to an existing one, a new deployment context).
* **Collect** required information from the system's business owner, technical team, data stewards, and any affected stakeholder groups.
* **Analyze** submitted information for risks to individuals and groups — bias and disparate impact, lack of explainability, insufficient human oversight, safety and robustness gaps, security vulnerabilities, and privacy risk arising from the system's data use.
* **Draft** AI risk and impact assessment reports and **route** material risks for human review.
* **Identify** the human-oversight mechanism required for each system given its risk tier, and **verify** it exists and is documented, not merely assumed.
* **Weigh** benefits alongside risks — an assessment states what the system is for and who it serves, not only what could go wrong.

#### Model & System Documentation

* **Maintain** technical documentation for each inventoried AI system: architecture, training data provenance and characteristics, intended purpose, performance metrics, known limitations, and version history.
* **Draft** model or system documentation records (model cards, system cards, or equivalent) suitable for both technical and non-technical audiences.
* **Maintain** a record of material changes to a model or system, including retraining, fine-tuning, and prompt or configuration changes that affect behavior.
* **Document** the data used to develop and evaluate each system, including known gaps, exclusions, and representativeness limitations.
* **Reconcile** documentation across systems and **identify** discrepancies between what a system is documented to do and what it is actually deployed to do.

#### Testing, Validation & Performance Monitoring

* **Track** testing, evaluation, verification, and validation (TEVV) activities performed on each AI system prior to deployment and on a recurring basis after.
* **Monitor** deployed systems for performance drift, degraded accuracy, and emerging bias or disparate-impact patterns.
* **Identify** systems overdue for re-evaluation given their risk tier and the interval defined for it.
* **Generate** monitoring summaries and **flag** systems whose observed performance no longer matches their documented characteristics.
* **Escalate** systems showing a material safety, fairness, or reliability regression.

#### Third-Party & Vendor AI Governance

* **Collect** AI governance information and documentation from vendors supplying AI systems, models, or AI-enabled features, including foundation-model providers.
* **Review** vendor AI documentation, model cards, evaluation results, and contractual AI provisions against defined requirements.
* **Identify** missing documentation, control gaps, and potential risks arising from reliance on a third-party model or system, including risk inherited from a vendor's own upstream dependencies.
* **Maintain** a third-party AI risk register and **track** remediation commitments.
* **Monitor** vendor assessment expiration dates and required re-assessments, especially after a vendor updates or replaces an underlying model.
* **Draft** AI governance requirements, vendor questionnaires, assessment summaries, and remediation communications.

#### AI Incident Management

* **Receive** AI-related incident notifications and **create** incident records — a safety failure, a harmful or biased output, a hallucination causing material reliance harm, or a system behaving outside its documented scope.
* **Collect** relevant incident facts: affected system, affected individuals or groups, what the system did, what a human relying on it did as a result, and known downstream impact.
* **Perform** a preliminary impact assessment using approved criteria.
* **Identify** potentially applicable notification, contractual, and regulatory requirements.
* **Track** incident-related deadlines, decisions, approvals, communications, and remediation activities.
* **Prepare** incident summaries and draft regulatory, customer, or internal communications for appropriate human review.
* **Escalate** incidents that meet predefined severity, safety, legal, or executive-reporting thresholds.

#### Regulatory & Standards Monitoring

* **Monitor** AI-specific laws, regulations, regulatory guidance, enforcement actions, and relevant standards developments (e.g. updates to the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and applicable state or sectoral AI laws).
* **Identify** regulatory or standards changes that may affect the organization's AI systems, procurement practices, contracts, or policies.
* **Summarize** developments and **map** affected requirements to existing AI governance controls and policies.
* **Create** implementation checklists and action plans for identified changes.
* **Track** effective dates, enforcement deadlines, and required organizational actions.

#### Policy & Documentation Management

* **Review** AI use policies, acceptable-use guidance, model-risk policies, and internal guidance for accuracy and consistency.
* **Identify** documents requiring updates based on regulatory changes, new system deployments, or assessment findings.
* **Draft** proposed policy revisions, AI use notices, procedures, and supporting documentation.
* **Maintain** document version history, approval records, review dates, and ownership information.
* **Compare** organizational AI practices against documented requirements and **identify** discrepancies.

#### Training & Awareness

* **Develop** AI governance training materials based on role, system exposure, regulatory requirements, and organizational policy.
* **Schedule** recurring AI governance training and awareness activities.
* **Track** employee completion and **identify** overdue training.
* **Generate** reminders, completion reports, and management summaries.
* **Answer** routine AI governance questions using approved policies, procedures, and knowledge resources.
* **Escalate** questions involving legal interpretation, significant risk, or exceptions to established policy.

#### Reporting & Executive Support

* **Compile** AI governance metrics from approved systems and records.
* **Calculate** program performance indicators, including assessment volumes, incident trends, remediation status, training completion, and overdue obligations.
* **Generate** executive dashboards and periodic AI governance reports.
* **Identify** emerging trends, recurring control failures, and material risk areas across the AI system inventory.
* **Prepare** briefing materials for executive leadership, board committees, audits, and regulatory engagements.
* **Maintain** an auditable record of AI governance program activities, decisions, approvals, and supporting evidence.

#### Administrative & Operational Tasks

* **Maintain** the AI governance compliance calendar and recurring task schedule.
* **Create** tickets, reminders, workflows, and follow-ups for AI governance activities.
* **Send** approved notifications and follow-up communications.
* **Organize** AI governance records and supporting documentation in designated systems.
* **Reconcile** AI governance records across systems and **identify** discrepancies.
* **Prepare** audit evidence packages and respond to routine information requests using approved documentation.
* **Monitor** external AI governance resources, subscriptions, certifications, and renewal dates.
* **Maintain** a current inventory of AI governance tools, vendors, resources, and program dependencies.

#### Human Escalation & Control

* **Escalate** legal interpretations, high-risk classifications, regulatory notifications, material incidents, policy exceptions, and decisions requiring executive authority.
* **Require** human approval before sending legally significant communications, making material risk-classification or conformity determinations, approving exceptions, or committing the organization to a regulatory position.
* **Never** let an AI system's own classification, assessment, or documentation stand as the final word on whether it may be deployed for a consequential use — a named accountable person makes that call.
* **Document** the rationale, evidence, approvals, and final disposition for material AI governance decisions.
* **Operate** within defined policies, approval thresholds, access controls, and audit requirements while serving as the organization's continuous AI-governance-program automation layer.
