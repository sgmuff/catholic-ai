# Framework registry

## eu-ai-act — EU AI Act — Risk Tiers & Obligations

- **Type:** law
- **Citation root:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
- **Review status:** reviewed
- **Source:** https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/
- **Trigger:** Applies to providers placing an AI system on the market or putting it into service in the EU, and to deployers of AI systems located in the EU — with extraterritorial reach to a provider or deployer outside the EU whose AI system's output is used within the EU (Art. 2). The obligations that attach depend entirely on which risk tier the system falls into: a prohibited practice under Art. 5 may not be placed on the market at all; a high-risk system under Art. 6 and Annex III triggers the full Chapter III obligation set; a system covered by Art. 50 triggers transparency-only obligations; everything else is minimal-risk with no tier-specific obligations under this Act.

- **Jurisdiction:** European Union, European Economic Area, Providers or deployers outside the EU/EEA whose AI system's output is used within the EU/EEA (extraterritorial scope, Art. 2)
- **File:** `frameworks/ai-governance/eu-ai-act.yaml`

## nist-ai-rmf — NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) 1.0

- **Type:** standard
- **Citation root:** NIST AI RMF 1.0
- **Review status:** reviewed
- **Source:** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **Trigger:** Voluntary — an institution opts into assessment against this framework to structure AI risk management using a common vocabulary, often alongside EU AI Act compliance work or a procurement requirement that references it. Not a checklist of binding requirements; each function's outcomes are adopted, adapted, or explicitly deprioritized with a stated reason, the same way the NIST Privacy Framework's profiles work.

- **Jurisdiction:** any
- **File:** `frameworks/ai-governance/nist-ai-rmf.yaml`

## iso-42001 — ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)

- **Type:** standard
- **Citation root:** ISO/IEC 42001:2023
- **Review status:** reviewed
- **Source:** https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
- **Trigger:** Voluntary — an institution opts into assessment against this standard, typically to structure an AI management system (AIMS) for organizations that develop, provide, or use AI systems, or to demonstrate AI governance maturity to partners, regulators, or auditors. Follows the same high-level structure (clauses 4-10) shared across other ISO management-system standards (e.g. ISO/IEC 27001), extended with an AI-specific Annex A control set.

- **Jurisdiction:** any
- **File:** `frameworks/ai-governance/iso-42001.yaml`
