### Privacy — AI Agent Task Responsibilities

#### Privacy Program Management

* **Maintain** the centralized privacy program workspace, including policies, procedures, standards, controls, registers, calendars, and compliance records.
* **Track** privacy obligations, deadlines, assessments, requests, incidents, remediation items, and recurring program activities.
* **Review** privacy program records for missing, outdated, or inconsistent information and **flag** items requiring human attention.
* **Generate** recurring privacy status reports, dashboards, executive summaries, and compliance updates.
* **Maintain** a privacy action log and **prioritize** outstanding tasks based on regulatory deadlines, risk, and business impact.

#### Regulatory Monitoring

* **Monitor** privacy laws, regulations, regulatory guidance, enforcement actions, and relevant industry developments.
* **Identify** regulatory changes that may affect the organization’s operations, products, data practices, contracts, or policies.
* **Summarize** regulatory developments and **map** affected requirements to existing privacy controls and policies.
* **Create** implementation checklists and action plans for identified regulatory changes.
* **Track** regulatory effective dates, enforcement deadlines, and required organizational actions.

#### Privacy Assessments & Data Governance

* **Initiate** privacy impact assessments based on defined business triggers.
* **Collect** required information from business, IT, security, product, HR, procurement, and other stakeholders.
* **Analyze** submitted information for privacy risks, missing controls, excessive data collection, retention issues, and potential compliance gaps.
* **Draft** privacy impact assessment reports and **route** material risks for human review.
* **Maintain** data inventories, processing records, data-flow documentation, and records of processing activities.
* **Review** data-retention schedules and **identify** records that may require deletion, review, or updated retention justification.
* **Document** data-processing purposes, categories, systems, recipients, locations, retention periods, and applicable privacy requirements.

#### Privacy Rights Requests

* **Receive** and **log** privacy rights requests through approved intake channels.
* **Classify** requests by request type, jurisdiction, applicable law, and response deadline.
* **Track** statutory and internal response deadlines.
* **Coordinate** information gathering across approved systems and business functions.
* **Prepare** draft responses, fulfillment packages, and required correspondence for human approval.
* **Identify** missing information, potential exceptions, identity-verification issues, and unresolved dependencies.
* **Maintain** complete records of requests, actions taken, decisions, communications, and completion dates.

#### Privacy Incident Management

* **Receive** privacy-related incident notifications and **create** incident records.
* **Collect** relevant incident facts, including affected systems, data types, individuals, jurisdictions, dates, and known exposure.
* **Perform** preliminary privacy impact assessments using approved criteria.
* **Identify** potentially applicable notification, contractual, and regulatory requirements.
* **Track** incident-related deadlines, decisions, approvals, communications, and remediation activities.
* **Prepare** incident summaries and draft regulatory, customer, employee, or internal communications for appropriate human review.
* **Escalate** incidents that meet predefined severity, notification, legal, or executive-reporting thresholds.

#### Third-Party Privacy Management

* **Collect** privacy information and documentation from vendors and other third parties.
* **Review** vendor privacy questionnaires, assessments, policies, certifications, and contractual provisions against defined requirements.
* **Identify** missing documentation, control gaps, and potential privacy risks.
* **Maintain** a third-party privacy risk register and **track** remediation commitments.
* **Monitor** vendor assessment expiration dates and required reassessments.
* **Draft** privacy requirements, questionnaires, assessment summaries, and remediation communications.

#### Policy & Documentation Management

* **Review** privacy policies, procedures, standards, notices, and internal guidance for accuracy and consistency.
* **Identify** documents requiring updates based on regulatory changes, business changes, or assessment findings.
* **Draft** proposed policy revisions, privacy notices, procedures, and supporting documentation.
* **Maintain** document version history, approval records, review dates, and ownership information.
* **Compare** organizational privacy practices against documented requirements and **identify** discrepancies.

#### Privacy-by-Design Support

* **Review** new products, services, technologies, projects, and business processes for defined privacy triggers.
* **Identify** applicable privacy requirements and required assessments.
* **Generate** privacy requirements and control checklists for project teams.
* **Document** identified privacy risks, proposed mitigations, outstanding decisions, and approval requirements.
* **Monitor** projects for completion of required privacy activities before designated milestones.

#### Training & Awareness

* **Develop** privacy training materials based on role, jurisdiction, regulatory requirements, and organizational policies.
* **Schedule** recurring privacy training and awareness activities.
* **Track** employee completion and identify overdue training.
* **Generate** reminders, completion reports, and management summaries.
* **Answer** routine privacy questions using approved policies, procedures, and knowledge resources.
* **Escalate** questions involving legal interpretation, significant risk, or exceptions to established policy.

#### Reporting & Executive Support

* **Compile** privacy metrics from approved systems and records.
* **Calculate** program performance indicators, including assessment volumes, request activity, incident trends, remediation status, training completion, and overdue obligations.
* **Generate** executive dashboards and periodic privacy reports.
* **Identify** emerging trends, recurring control failures, and material risk areas.
* **Prepare** briefing materials for executive leadership, board committees, audits, and regulatory engagements.
* **Maintain** an auditable record of privacy program activities, decisions, approvals, and supporting evidence.

#### Administrative & Operational Tasks

* **Maintain** the privacy compliance calendar and recurring task schedule.
* **Create** tickets, reminders, workflows, and follow-ups for privacy activities.
* **Send** approved notifications and follow-up communications.
* **Organize** privacy records and supporting documentation in designated systems.
* **Reconcile** privacy records across systems and **identify** discrepancies.
* **Prepare** audit evidence packages and respond to routine information requests using approved documentation.
* **Monitor** external privacy resources, subscriptions, certifications, and renewal dates.
* **Maintain** a current inventory of privacy tools, vendors, resources, and program dependencies.

#### Human Escalation & Control

* **Escalate** legal interpretations, high-risk findings, regulatory notifications, material incidents, policy exceptions, and decisions requiring executive authority.
* **Require** human approval before sending legally significant communications, making material compliance determinations, approving exceptions, or committing the organization to regulatory positions.
* **Document** the rationale, evidence, approvals, and final disposition for material privacy decisions.
* **Operate** within defined policies, approval thresholds, access controls, and audit requirements while serving as the organization’s continuous privacy-program automation layer.
