# Projects for secular institutions

Work in this track is written for institutions that do not share the Church's starting point but do, in practice, share many of its conclusions — companies, public agencies, and other secular organizations governing their own use of AI. These projects argue from the same underlying commitments as the Catholic-institutions track — subsidiarity, solidarity, the common good, the dignity owed to a person who is never merely a means — but in terms a compliance officer or board can act on without first being persuaded of the theology beneath them. The rigor is the same; only the register changes.
