# Projects for Catholic institutions

Work in this track is written for dioceses, religious orders, Catholic universities, hospitals, and charities — institutions that already share the Church's account of the human person, and so don't need that account argued from first principles. Projects here can speak in explicitly confessional terms: citing the Catechism, papal and conciliar teaching, and canon law directly, in service of concrete governance work — policy, risk assessment, model documentation — rather than catechesis for its own sake.

Empty for now. Projects land here one at a time; see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how.
